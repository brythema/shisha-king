Good questions.

Who can we serve?

Adults aged 18 and above only. We may ask for ID at the event — no exceptions.

Which areas do you cover?

Everywhere in Abuja — Maitama, Wuse II, Asokoro, Gwarinpa, Jabi, Kubwa and beyond. Homes, hotels, lounges and outdoor venues. Further out? Just ask.

How early should I book?

As early as you can. Weekends and December go first — some dates fill weeks ahead.

What do you bring?

Pipes, flavours, coals, burners, disposable mouthpieces, attendants and fire safety equipment. We also clean up after.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes — a deposit secures your date. Full details come with your quote.
