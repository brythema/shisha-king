01 / The opportunity

More than shisha.
A brand people remember.

Shisha King brings the shisha lounge to the event. Rather than relying on a permanent venue, the business travels to its customers with equipment, flavours, trained attendants and a visually distinctive branded station. Each event can generate revenue today and brand-building content for tomorrow.

The ambition is to build a recognisable Abuja event and lifestyle brand — one customers actively want at their celebrations, rather than simply choosing the cheapest supplier.

13 / The investment case

Build a brand,
not just a service.

Shisha King combines a mobile service business with a deliberate brand-building system. Equipment creates delivery capacity; branded presentation creates visibility; events create content; content builds recognition; and recognition can support future bookings.

THE PRODUCT

A real service

A tangible event experience with defined packages, delivery standards and repeatable operations.

THE DIFFERENCE

Visible branding

Uniforms and a modular station make the service identifiable at the venue and in approved event content.

THE UPSIDE

Compounding media

A growing library of event stories can strengthen the brand and create partnership opportunities.

The final investor proposal should add verified equipment quotes, full startup capital, cost per event, package prices, contribution margin, revenue scenarios, break-even analysis and proposed investment or partnership terms. No revenue or profit outcome is guaranteed.
