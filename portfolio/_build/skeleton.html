01 / Why it's a good idea

The lounge comes
to the event.

Abuja's event economy — weddings, milestone birthdays, private parties — is large, social and always moving. Shisha service already sits inside it, but most of it is ad hoc: unbranded equipment, inconsistent service, and nothing that makes a brand stick. That gap is the opportunity.

Shisha King answers it with a mobile, fully branded service. Instead of a permanent lounge, the experience travels to the client's venue: equipment, a flavour menu, trained attendants and a distinctive branded station. Each event earns revenue today and creates the content that earns recognition tomorrow.

08 / Signature packages

Pick your throne.

Three packages establish the positioning. Exact quantities, service hours and pricing get confirmed against supplier quotes and operating costs before launch.

01 · ESSENTIAL

The Crown

A refined setup for intimate gatherings. Selected shishas, a core flavour menu, agreed service time and branded presentation.

02 · PREMIUM

The Royal

More pipes, a broader flavour menu, dedicated service and enhanced presentation for a larger gathering.

03 · SIGNATURE

The Kingdom

A statement experience with expanded capacity, multiple attendants where required and a custom VIP-style setup.

Potential add-ons: additional shishas, extra service hours, extra attendants, upgraded presentation and customised event arrangements. All offers remain subject to venue approval and safe operating conditions.
Product depth

The King's Pick

Featured blends and equipment stories are part of the idea, not an afterthought. A signature product line gives the content engine a recurring subject — and gives events a reason to remember the brand long after the last pipe is packed away.

13 / Why it's worth exploring

A brand,
not just a service.

What separates this idea from another event-service pitch: a mobile service with a deliberate brand system attached to it. Equipment creates delivery capacity; branded presentation creates visibility; events create content; content builds recognition; and recognition feeds future bookings.

THE PRODUCT

A real service

A tangible event experience with defined packages, delivery standards and repeatable operations.

THE DIFFERENCE

Uniforms and a modular station make the service identifiable at the venue and in approved event content.

THE COMPOUND EFFECT

Compounding media

A growing library of event stories strengthens the brand and creates partnership opportunities over time.
